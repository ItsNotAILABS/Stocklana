# Stocklana V2 — Product Experience

Stocklana V2 is action-first. The product opens on user intent rather than infrastructure.

## Immediate jobs

1. **Buy** a tokenized equity 24/7.
2. **Auto-invest** through recurring buys, baskets or robo allocation.
3. **Borrow** through funded Stocklana credit or supported Kamino routes.
4. **Play a market** using fully collateralized programmable instruments.
5. **Send** through MAQUE handles, QR or wallet rails.
6. **Spend** through governed one-use card / payment policy surfaces.
7. **Agent**: provision an AI with its own Agent Vault and bounded authority.
8. **Launch** through the embedded ETH/Solana launch exchange.

## V2 financial substrate

All user-facing programmable financial receipts default to Token-2022 profiles. The earlier V1 issuance path is removed from the V2 product surface and source package. The same double-entry accounting grammar powers human and agent state.

## Progressive disclosure

Infrastructure remains inspectable under **System**, but it is never required reading before a user can act. Every advanced subsystem appears first as a concrete use: send, spend, borrow, automate, delegate, launch or prove.
